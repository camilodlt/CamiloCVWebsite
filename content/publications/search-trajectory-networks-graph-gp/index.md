abstract: Cartesian Genetic Programming allows for the optimization of interpretable function representations, but understanding its vast and combinatorially complex search space remains challenging, especially because multiple genotypes may correspond to identical functions. This paper studies the application of Search Trajectory Networks to understand the search dynamics of Cartesian Genetic Programming for symbolic regression tasks. Using Search Trajectory Networks, it analyzes the behavior of evolutionary search processes and highlights phenomena such as portal minima, critical points that enable sudden beneficial shifts in the search trajectory. The results suggest that a functional analysis based on Search Trajectory Networks can provide clearer insight into search behavior than a purely genetic interpretation.
