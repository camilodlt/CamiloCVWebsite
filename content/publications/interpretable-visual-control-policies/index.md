abstract: This paper proposes a graph-based genetic programming approach to evolve inherently interpretable policies for vision-based sequential decision-making tasks. Instead of relying on black-box neural-network policies and post hoc explanations, the method builds compact policies from transparent operational modules, making the decision process directly understandable. Evaluated on Atari benchmarks, the work shows that interpretable policies can provide a more faithful understanding of behavior while remaining competitive on challenging control tasks.
