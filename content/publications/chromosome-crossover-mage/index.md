abstract: Crossover operators have historically shown limited effectiveness in Cartesian Genetic Programming, with mutation-only approaches typically dominating the evolutionary process. This paper explores a natural crossover mechanism enabled by Multimodal Adaptive Graph Evolution, a multi-chromosome generalization of Cartesian Genetic Programming that groups functions by return type and constrains graph mutation based on type coherence. The work implements and evaluates a chromosome-level crossover operator on selected problems from the Second Program Synthesis Benchmark Suite, comparing it against a mutation-only strategy. The experiments show mixed effectiveness overall, with promising exceptions on problems such as GCD and Twitter.
